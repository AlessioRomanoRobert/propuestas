01 Treatments

What we do at the clinic

General dentistry for the whole family. Every mouth is different, so we don't publish prices. Book a visit and we'll explain.

  1. Check-up and first visit

    We look at your teeth and gums and tell you what we see, calmly.

  2. Dental cleaning

    Professional hygiene to keep your mouth healthy between check-ups.

  3. Emergencies

    Toothache or something broken? Message us and we'll tell you when you can come in.

  4. Orthodontics

    We assess the position of your teeth and explain the options.

  5. Oral surgery and extractions

    When needed, explained beforehand and followed up afterwards.

  6. Fixed and removable prosthetics

    Crowns, bridges and removable dentures.

  7. Implants

    Implant-supported prosthetics: we walk you through the process step by step.

  8. X-rays

    Dental X-rays and panoramic (OPG) X-rays.

Prices: book a visit and we'll explain.
