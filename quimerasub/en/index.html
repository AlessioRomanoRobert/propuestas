02 Radazul in metres

The deep starts a few fin kicks away

On Tenerife's east coast the seabed drops quickly: there's room for first-timers and for technical divers, without going far from the marina.

  1. 0 mDeparture from the marina and entry from the shore.
  2. 6 mMaximum depth of the try dive.
  3. 12 mAreas suited to beginners.
  4. 20 mMaximum depth of the Open Water course.
  5. 10–30 mEl Emisario de Radazul: trumpetfish, schools of bogue, rays and barracudas.
  6. 40 m+Volcanic walls a short way from the beach: technical diving territory.
15–40 mvisibility
20–24 °Cwater, winter to summer

Common marine life

Ornate wrasse, damselfish, seabream, salema, moray eels, octopus and cuttlefish. Sometimes barracudas, rays, amberjacks and the odd turtle (not guaranteed).

Sources: radazul.org and PADI's Radazul dive site page.

07 FAQ

Do I need experience for the try dive?

No. The try dive is for people who have never dived: first a briefing on land, then a 30–45 minute dive to a maximum of 6 metres.

What should I bring?

The club provides the diving gear. Bring swimwear and a towel; there are changing rooms with showers. We'll confirm everything on WhatsApp.

What if the weather is bad?

The club decides each day whether diving is possible and where, depending on wind, swell and visibility. If it isn't safe, we find another date.

Can children come?

It depends on their age and the activity. Message us and we'll tell you the options.

Can I fly after diving?

You shouldn't fly or go up to altitude until 24 hours after your last dive. Keep it in mind if you're leaving the island or going up Mount Teide.

How do I pay?

We'll explain when we confirm your booking on WhatsApp.
