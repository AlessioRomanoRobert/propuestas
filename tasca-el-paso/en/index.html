03The place

An old-school local bar, with room to sit down.

A long stone-clad bar with a wooden top and a row of stools. Above it, amber globe lamps; at the back, the wine shelves and a chalkboard written by hand.

Across the room, wooden tables, long benches and an old cart wheel on the wall. Plenty of space for morning coffee, lunch or an evening tapa.
