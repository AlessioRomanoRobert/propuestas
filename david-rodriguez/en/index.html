Our story

Self-taught, from Santa Úrsula

Jesús David Rodríguez was born in Santa Úrsula and learned from his mother, who made the desserts at the family restaurant, the “Rodríguez”. That restaurant later became a pastry workshop, where he made his first desserts with his parents.

He trained at the Hofmann Hospitality School, in France with Vallhonrat chocolates and, for ice cream, with José Manuel Marcos Candela, Spanish Ice Cream Champion 2011.

His style: traditional French or Catalan patisserie, “with his own design touch”.

  1. 2017Opens in Santa Úrsula: “Since 2017, offering our sweetest side.”
  2. 2018Best Work in Pastry at the 33rd Diario de Avisos National Gastronomy Awards.
  3. 2024Wins the 7th Canary Islands Pastry Championship (Grupo Harinalia Grand Prize, GastroCanarias).
  4. 2025Ice-cream parlour and patisserie at Columbus Plaza, by Plaza del Charco.
  5. 2026Opens at Nivaria Center in Santa Cruz, with interiors by Manuel Martín.
