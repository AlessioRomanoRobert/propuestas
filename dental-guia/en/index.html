01The clinic

A small, bright practice right on the street.

On Avenida Isora, at number 74: white façade, blue sign and the orange «Pide tu cita» circles on the glass. Inside, a reception with orchids, fern wallpaper and a quiet waiting room.

The practice is led by Dr Violeta Culev, general dentistry. In their reviews, patients mention the doctor and her team by name, which says a lot about how things are done here.
