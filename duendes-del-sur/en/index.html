The murals

Every wall
tells a story.

Goblins in the lounge, fairies in the women's dorm, Klimt's The Kiss in a double room and a seabed full of divers on the bathroom ceiling. The murals are the work of artist Oskars Ouk.

The house

A garden in the shade of a giant fig tree.

The hostel revolves around the courtyard: a fig tree shading everything, a colourful hammock beneath it and wooden cabins all around. After the beach, this is where people cook, chat and have a cold beer.

Around us

Snorkelling, surf and whales within a stroll.

  • 10 min walk

    Montaña Amarilla

    A hill of yellow volcanic ash by the sea. Underneath: arches and lava caves, one of the best snorkelling spots on the island.

  • Behind the hostel

    The cliffs

    A coastal path with quiet coves, sunbathing spots and steps down into the water.

  • 10–15 min walk

    Las Galletas harbour

    A fishing village with a beach, a promenade and boat trips to see whales, dolphins and turtles. Diving, kayaking and sailing.

  • 3 km

    Surf at Las Galletas

    The nearest surf spot. Las Américas is 17 km away.

  • 11 km

    Los Abrigos

    Natural rock pools in the volcanic coastline.

  • 17 km

    El Médano

    Windsurfing, kitesurfing and a laid-back, boho vibe.

  • 49 km

    Mount Teide

    The 3,718 m volcano, for a day trip.

Getting here

A white wall
and a painted sign.

Calle Polifemo, 5 · 38630 Costa del Silencio · Arona

  1. From Tenerife South airport

    About 12 km. Around €20 by taxi.

  2. By bus

    Line 111 towards Los Cristianos as far as Guaza; there, change to the 467 towards Costa del Silencio and get off at Ten-Bel.

  3. The last few metres

    Head for Calle Polifemo. Once you pass the old tennis courts on your left, turn left: four steps down and, ten metres on, the door is on your right.

  4. When you arrive

    Ring the bell. Arriving at night? Call us and we'll let you in.

Before you come

Quick answers

  • What time can I arrive?

    Check-in is from 2 pm to 11 pm, every day. If your flight is delayed, message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can.

  • And check-out?

    By 11 am.

  • Is breakfast included?

    No, but the kitchen is free for everyone and there's a supermarket about 5 minutes' walk away.

  • Sheets and towels?

    Bed linen is included. Towels can be rented at reception.

  • How do I pay?

    On arrival, in cash or by card.

  • Is there a female-only room?

    Yes: Las Hadas, a 4-bed dorm with a lockable door.

  • Are pets allowed?

    Sorry, no.

  • What if I need to cancel?

    We'll explain the conditions when we confirm your booking.
