02Dive sites

Ten dives minutes from the harbour

Depth, current and level of each site, as Gonzalo and Carlos describe them. The site of the day depends on the sea.

Depth

Maravilla12+ m

Current: NoneLevel: Try dive · Open Water

Our go-to cove for try dives… and where we've seen the most seahorses. Perfect for your last day: you can make your tank last and last.

Montaña Amarilla18+ m

Current: LowLevel: Open Water · Advanced

The queen of volcanic formations: from its fossil dune to La Puerta, a collapsed mountain. Sheltered from wind and current – the site that never fails.

Los Chuchos8–21 m

Current: MediumLevel: Open Water

“Ray city”, the star dive of southern Tenerife. You dive among rays and next to a sunken boat.

Cueva del Tiburón18+ m

Current: Medium/highLevel: Open Water · Advanced

Crystal-clear water and so much life it feels like an aquarium in the open sea. When conditions allow, one of the best spots for a try dive.

Arco de Coral18+ m

Current: Medium/highLevel: Open Water · Advanced

A few minutes away by boat: basalt formations shaped into arches made for photos.

El Palmar25+ m

Current: Medium/highLevel: Open Water · Advanced

Next to Los Cristianos. Its cave is one of the few spots where seeing turtles is almost certain.

El Champiñón14–32 m

Current: Medium/highLevel: Advanced

San Miguel25+ m

Current: Medium/highLevel: Advanced · 30+ logged dives

One of the liveliest sites: everything has been seen here, from devil rays to dolphins.

El Tanque25+ m

Current: Medium/highLevel: Advanced · 60+ logged dives

For experienced divers: alleys, schools of barracuda, rays, amberjacks and coral. Dive with Carlos (Huesi) and he'll introduce you to the moray that has lived at 32 metres for over 20 years.

Coral Negro25+ m

Basalt and black coral. Unmissable if you dive in the south of Tenerife.

08FAQ

Do I need experience for the try dive?

No. First a short briefing on land: equalising your ears, breathing from the regulator, signals and safety rules. Then we go out by boat and your instructor stays with you the whole time.

How long will I be underwater?

We don't set a time limit: if you're comfortable, you stay as long as your tank lasts, which depends on how you breathe.

What should I bring?

Swimwear and a towel. The centre has a hot shower, washbasin and lockers. We'll confirm the rest on WhatsApp.

What if I struggle to equalise?

Every try diver has their own instructor and goes at their own pace. If it doesn't work out on the day, we'll give you tips to try again.

What if the weather is bad?

Each day we choose the most sheltered site. If it isn't safe to dive, we'll suggest another date.

Can children come?

It depends on their age and the activity. Message us and we'll tell you the options.

Can I fly after diving?

Wait 24 hours after your last dive before flying or going up to altitude – Mount Teide included.
