Tenerife HostBefore you gop. 06

No. 06 — Before you go

Good to know

01What's included?

On the Grand Tour and the Magic Circle (per Viator): private air-conditioned transport, hotel pickup and drop-off, fuel and parking, bottled water and a private guide. Not included: lunch, gratuities and the Teide cable car, which is optional (€27 per adult, €13.50 per child aged 3–13). For the other tours, we'll confirm on WhatsApp.

02What if the weather is bad?

If the weather doesn't cooperate, you're offered another date or a full refund.

03What's the cancellation policy?

Full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours in advance. Within 24 hours there are no refunds or changes.

04Is it suitable for children?

Infant seats are available and there's no published minimum age. Not recommended for pregnant travellers or anyone with heart problems, and not wheelchair accessible. Any doubts, ask us on WhatsApp.

05What should I bring?

A warm layer for Mount Teide, where it's usually much cooler than on the coast, plus sunglasses, sun cream and comfortable shoes. Bottled water is included.

06How do I pay?

For direct bookings, guests describe a payment link by PayPal or card. We'll confirm on WhatsApp.

07Which language is the tour in?

English, which Gregory speaks excellently. For other languages, please ask.

08Is the price per person or per group?

Viator shows the “from” price per adult; other platforms list it per group. Message us with your numbers and Gregory will give you a fixed price.
